Promote your visual novel for free with game jams

Jul 28, 2026 · For creators

Paid ads are expensive. Social posts disappear in a day. For indie visual novel and interactive fiction creators, one of the strongest free ways to promote your project and grow your audience is still underrated: join a game jam (or an asset jam if you sell packs).

On Arleco, jams are not just a side event — they are a discovery engine. Live jams get free promotion on the Discover page, and readers show up because they can read entries for free during the jam. That combination is a ready-made hook for new fans.

Why game jams beat cold social promotion

When you post a series link alone, you are asking strangers to pay attention (and sometimes Ducats) with no context. A jam gives them a reason: a theme, a deadline, a community feed, and a promise that trying your story is low-risk while the event is live.

  • Built-in attention — people browsing Discover already see active jams.
  • Lower friction — free reading during the jam removes the biggest first-chapter barrier.
  • Shared context — the jam theme helps readers understand what your series is about before they click.
  • Zero ad spend — your time goes into a playable entry, not paid reach.

How Arleco jams help you grow an audience

1. Free promotion on Discover

Popular and live jams appear where readers already look for something new. Instead of fighting the algorithm on every platform, you ride Arleco’s own browse surface — the same place featured series and continue-reading sit.

2. Free-to-read as a marketing hook

Readers are attracted to the fact they can play for free during the jam. Use that intentionally: enter a polished early chapter (or a jam-length route) that ends on a question, a cliffhanger, or a relationship beat that makes them want chapter two after the event.

3. Convert jam readers into series followers

Treat the jam entry as a trailer that lives inside your real series. After the jam, your paid or later chapters already have a warmer audience — people who finished something of yours once.

A simple jam promotion playbook

  1. Pick a jam that fits your tone — romance, horror, mystery, or open theme. Browse current events on the game jams page.
  2. Ship one complete, readable path — quality beats length. A tight 10–20 minute route outperforms an unfinished sprawl.
  3. Lead with chapter energy — strong opening dialogue, clear choices, and a memorable character beat.
  4. End with a hook — leave a reason to continue the series after free jam access ends.
  5. Share the jam URL, not only your series URL — friends who join via the jam page see the full shelf of entries and may discover you in context.

Asset jams: promotion for creators who sell packs

If you build characters, stages, UI kits, or audio packs, asset jams put shoppable work in front of other creators. The same logic applies: event attention + shop shelf = free discovery for your marketplace listings.

FAQ: using jams to market a visual novel

Do I need a finished series to join?

No. A strong first chapter or jam-specific route is enough. Many creators use jams specifically to launch series one.

Is joining a jam really free promotion?

On Arleco, yes for the platform side: live jams are surfaced on Discover, and free reading during the jam is the reader incentive. Your “cost” is creative time — which also improves your catalog.

What if my series is paid after the jam?

That is the point of the hook. Free during the jam gets strangers in the door; later chapters can use your normal monetization once they care about the cast and plot.
